tronworks studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Summary

tronworks studio is a private tool that manages TikTok accounts owned by tronworks. It runs on the operator's own computer. It does not collect data about other TikTok users, and it does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

What the tool receives from TikTok

  • Profile basics (user.info.basic): the connected account's open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: issued by TikTok when the owner connects an account, used only to upload and publish that account's own posts (video.upload, video.publish).

Where it is kept

Everything is stored locally on the operator's computer: tokens in a protected local file and profile basics in a local database. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

What it is not used for

No advertising, no profiling, no analytics on other people, no sharing with third parties.

Deleting data

Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its tokens and profile data. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by its adult operator.

Changes and contact

This policy may change; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: open an issue at github.com/tronworks/tronworks.github.io/issues. See also the Terms of Service.
