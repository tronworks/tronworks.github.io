What tronworks studio is

tronworks studio is a private content-management tool run by tronworks. It drafts, schedules and publishes posts for a small number of TikTok accounts that tronworks owns and operates. It is not offered to the public, and no one can sign up for it.

Who may use it

Only the operator of tronworks may use the tool, and only with TikTok accounts the operator owns. Connecting an account through "Login with TikTok" means you are that account's owner and agree to these terms.

AI-generated content

The accounts managed with this tool are openly AI personas. Their content is AI-generated and is labelled as such, in the profile and on posts, as TikTok's rules require. The personas are fictional and are never made to resemble a real person.

Your responsibilities

Content published through the tool must follow the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The tool only uses TikTok's official APIs. It never creates accounts, never automates engagement (likes, follows, comments), and never posts without the operator's approval.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect an account at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, or from within the tool.
